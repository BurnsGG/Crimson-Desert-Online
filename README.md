# CrimsonZero

**A new online experience for Crimson Desert.** A multiplayer framework with persistent servers, a shared world, and a scripting platform anyone can build on.

> ⚠️ **Early development.** Nothing here is playable yet. This repository is shared so people can follow the progress, give feedback and get involved early.

- **Discord:** https://discord.gg/268w3bxDMV

---

## What is CrimsonZero?

Crimson Desert was first revealed as an online game. That online part never came. CrimsonZero is a community project to build a new kind of online experience for it, in the spirit of what FiveM did for GTA V.

- **Persistent servers.** One world that lives on: your character, your gear, your money and the world itself are kept by the server, not by your local save.
- **A shared world.** Meet, trade, fight and team up with other players.
- **A platform, not just a game mode.** Server owners and creators build their own content: jobs, events, quests, economies, whole game modes.

**This is not a co-op mod.** It's a separate online experience with its own rules.

**It doesn't replace the single-player game.** You choose when to play online. Your single-player game and its saves stay exactly as they are.

## The vision

Pywel is one of the most beautiful worlds ever made, and right now you explore it alone. CrimsonZero imagines it full of other players: people with their own stories, goals and reputations. A world where what you do matters because someone else sees it, remembers it, or pays for it.

- **A world with consequences**
- **A player-driven economy**
- **Reasons to come together**
- **A world built by its community**
